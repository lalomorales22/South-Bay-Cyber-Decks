# Case Catalog

Six families. Six form factors. Mix them.

A case family is a *look and build style*. A form factor is *how it
opens and carries*. They are independent — a Sleek book and a Tactical
clamshell are both normal orders.

## Families

### 1. Vintage Terminal
Old-school computer. Beige / cream, chunky bezels, visible screws,
typewriter keys. The deck that looks like it belongs in a museum and
works like it belongs on your desk.
- Materials: ABS / printed shell, metal badge
- Pairs with: Typewriter wedge, SB-Type keyboard, round caps
- Trade-off: bigger, heavier, unmistakable

### 2. Sleek
Thin aluminum, minimal seams, muted colors. The deck you can open in a
coffee shop without starting a conversation — unless you want one.
- Materials: CNC / bent aluminum, acrylic window optional
- Pairs with: Foldable clamshell, SB-60 / SB-65, low-profile caps
- Trade-off: less room for big antennas / batteries

### 3. Tactical / Field
Rugged, handles, sealed edges, mounting points. For field work, ham
radio, and off-grid days. Drop it, splash it, keep working.
- Materials: Pelican-style shell, aluminum frame, rubber seals
- Pairs with: Luggable, SB-65 / SB-75, chunky caps, LoRa + GPS
- Trade-off: heaviest family, most expensive seals

### 4. Hidden / Stealth
Looks like a book, a briefcase, or a toolbox. Closed, nobody knows.
Open, it's a full computer. Discreet carry first.
- Materials: Book cloth / leatherette, briefcase shell, hidden hinges
- Pairs with: Book form, slab + detachable keyboard, SB-40 / SB-50
- Trade-off: thermals and screen size are constrained by the disguise

### 5. Exposed / Cyberpunk
Open frame, visible boards, brass and acrylic. A showpiece for maker
fairs and your desk. If you want people to ask questions, start here.
- Materials: Acrylic + brass standoffs, open aluminum frame
- Pairs with: Slab, SB-Macro, backlit caps, ESP32 + LCD visible
- Trade-off: no weather protection, dust is a lifestyle

### 6. Luggable
Suitcase-style, bigger screen, bigger battery. A portable workstation,
not a pocket toy. The one you bring when the work matters more than
the weight.
- Materials: Suitcase shell, aluminum frame, carry handle
- Pairs with: Luggable form, SB-75, extra battery, big antennas
- Trade-off: you will feel it in your shoulder

## Form factors

| Form | How it works | Best screen | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foldable clamshell | Laptop-style hinge, screen folds onto keyboard | 10″–13″ | Fixed, SB-60/65 |
| Slab + detachable | Tablet slab, keyboard detaches / magnet-mounts | 7″–13″ | Detachable, SB-40–65 |
| Typewriter wedge | All-in-one wedge, screen tilted at back | 7″–10″ | Fixed, SB-Type |
| Luggable / suitcase | Case opens, screen in lid, deck in base | 10″–13″ | Fixed, SB-65/75 |
| Split two-piece | Keyboard splits, screen unit separate | 7″–10″ | SB-Split36 |
| Book / hidden | Disguised cover, opens like a book | 7″–10″ | Slim, SB-40/50 |

## Customization beyond family + form

- Color / finish, engraving, nameplate
- Handle, shoulder strap, mounting rails
- Venting and fan vs. passive cooling (Orin Nano builds need this call)
- Hinge stiffness, latch type, feet, carry pouch

Tell us the bag, pelican case, or backpack it must fit — we design to
that dimension first, then to the look.
