Why not just buy a kit?

Because kits give you one mini keyboard and one box. Cyberdecks are personal — your deck should fit your hands, your bag, and your work. That's the whole business.

Vintage Terminal

Old-school computer energy. Beige, chunky, typewriter keys, chunky switches. Looks like it time-traveled from 1983.

Sleek

Thin aluminum, minimal seams, quiet. The deck you can take to a coffee shop without a conversation starting — unless you want one.

Pairs with: foldable clamshell, low-profile caps

Tactical / Field

Rugged, handles, sealed edges, big battery. Built for field work, ham radio, and off-grid days.

Pairs with: luggable, chunky tactile caps
📕

Hidden / Stealth

Looks like a book, a briefcase, or a toolbox. Closed, nobody knows. Open, it's a full computer.

Pairs with: book form, slab + detachable keyboard
⚡

Exposed / Cyberpunk

Open frame, visible boards, brass and acrylic. A showpiece for maker fairs and your desk.

Pairs with: slab, backlit caps
🧳

Luggable

Suitcase-style, bigger screen, bigger battery. A portable workstation, not a pocket toy.

Pairs with: luggable, 65% / 75% board

The keyboard is the crucial part. Every deck here uses a custom keyboard PCB — not the one mini keyboard everyone else reuses. We start from 10 cut-to-fit base boards, trim the outline to your case, place the keys where you want them, and add a touchpad only if you want one. How it works




1. You build it here
Configurator → Build Code. No account, no payment yet.


2. We confirm it
We check fit, power, thermals, and keyboard layout, then send a final quote + build sheet.


3. We build it
Case, custom keyboard PCB, wiring, test, burn-in. You get photos along the way.


4. It ships
With its build sheet and keyboard map, so you (or we) can repair and mod it later.
